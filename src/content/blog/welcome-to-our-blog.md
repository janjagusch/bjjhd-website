Welcome to the very first post on the **BJJ Heidelberg** blog! We are excited to
have a place to share what is happening around the club, beyond training times
and sign-up forms.

## What we will write about

This blog is where we will post:

- **Club news** – events, seminars, belt promotions and open-mat announcements.
- **Training tips** – small pointers to help you get more out of every session.
- **Stories from the mat** – competition recaps and highlights from our members.

## New to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?

If you have never trained before, don't worry. Our classes are beginner
friendly and you can [book a free trial class](/#trial) any time. Curious what a
first session looks like? Read the [beginner's guide](/en/beginners).

> Clean Jiu-Jitsu, mutual respect and having fun on the mat come first – not the
> ego.

See you on the mat!
